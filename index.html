ML Capstone Platform

A self-hosted classroom cluster: one shared LLM endpoint, GPU-backed speech-to-text, and a push-to-deploy pipeline where tests gate the deploy. Student setup guides, the first-deploy lab, the admin runbook, and symptom-first troubleshooting.

Virtual Patient Clinic

School of Medicine faculty

A voice-based standardized-patient simulator for the BYU School of Medicine pilot. One-page introduction for faculty: what it does, how a session runs, and what the pilot is trying to find out.

quinnsnell.github.io/virtual-patient-clinic/

Canvas Schedule Planner

Canvas instructors

A semester planner for Canvas LMS with one row per class meeting. Drag an assignment to a different day and its Canvas due date follows; add rich-text reading notes and material links per meeting; group days into named modules. The row-per-meeting layout Canvas itself does not offer.

quinnsnell.github.io/CanvasSchedulePlugin/
